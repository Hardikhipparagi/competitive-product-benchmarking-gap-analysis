# Competitive Product Benchmarking & Gap Analysis Tool

## Project Overview
An Excel-based Product Management and Competitive Intelligence project that compares a SaaS product with relevant competitors, identifies meaningful product gaps, and converts evidence into roadmap decisions.

## Business Problem
Product teams need a structured way to understand where their product is stronger or weaker than competitors without blindly copying every competitor feature.

## Objective
Benchmark TaskFlow against Asana, ClickUp, monday.com, Notion and Trello across features, pricing, customer experience, integrations, AI, security and support.

## Product Scenario
**Product:** TaskFlow  
**Category:** Productivity SaaS  
**Analysis type:** Illustrative portfolio benchmark

## Competitors Selected
- Asana
- ClickUp
- monday.com
- Notion
- Trello

## Dataset
The workbook contains 120 benchmarking records across 20 features and six products, plus feature matrix, gap analysis, opportunity analysis, roadmap and dashboard sheets.

## Methodology
1. Define product and market.
2. Select direct competitors.
3. Define benchmarking criteria.
4. Collect/structure competitor attributes.
5. Score feature availability using Yes = 100%, Partial = 50%, No = 0%.
6. Calculate feature coverage and competitive KPIs.
7. Identify gaps and advantages.
8. Prioritize opportunities.
9. Build a Now–Next–Later roadmap.
10. Present findings in an executive dashboard.

## Core KPIs
- Feature Coverage %
- Competitive Benchmark Score
- Customer Experience Score
- Pricing Competitiveness Score
- Product Gap Score
- Competitive Advantage Score
- Opportunity Score
- Customer Demand Score
- Product Rating
- High-Priority Gaps

## Key Insights
- TaskFlow has strong usability, pricing and core workflow positioning.
- AI, automation and enterprise-security capabilities are the largest strategic gaps.
- High-demand gaps are prioritized instead of copying every competitor feature.
- AI Assistant, AI Summaries and Automation are NOW opportunities.
- SSO & Enterprise Security, Advanced Reporting and Task Dependencies are NEXT opportunities.
- Audit Logs and AI-driven competitive alerts are LATER opportunities.
